Pushup Tracker Support

Help with the Pushup Tracker iPhone app

Found a bug or have a question? Open a support request and describe what happened. Include your iPhone model and iOS version if you can. (You'll need a free GitHub account.)

Common questions

The camera isn't counting my reps.
Prop your phone up so the camera can see the right body parts: shoulders, elbows and wrists for push-ups; hips, knees and feet for squats; your whole body for jumping jacks. For sit-ups, turn side-on to the camera. Good lighting helps. You can always tap the screen to count a rep instead.

The app says camera access is denied.
Open iOS Settings, scroll down to Pushup Tracker and turn on Camera.

How do I move my data to a new phone?
Your data is stored only on your iPhone. Use the backup export in the app to save a file, then import it on the new phone.

How do I add the widget?
Open the app once, then long-press your home screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Pushup Tracker.

Reminders aren't showing up.
Check that notifications are allowed for Pushup Tracker in iOS Settings, and that reminders are turned on in the app.
