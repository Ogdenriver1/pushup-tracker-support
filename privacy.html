Privacy Policy

Pushup Tracker · Last updated October 6, 2026

Short version: Pushup Tracker doesn't collect, store or share your personal data. Everything you log stays on your iPhone.

Your workout data

Your reps, goals, streaks, notes, challenges and settings are saved only on your device. There are no accounts and no servers that receive this data. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it. If you use the app's backup export, the file goes wherever you choose to send it; we never see it.

Camera

If you turn on camera counting, the camera is used to detect your body position and count reps. The video is processed live on your device. It is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

Location and weather

If you allow location access, the app looks up the current temperature and humidity when you start a workout, so you can see how weather affects your workouts. To do this, your location coordinates are sent to the free weather service Open-Meteo for that single request. The weather reading and location are saved on your device with your sets. We don't receive or store your location. Location access is optional and you can turn it off in iOS Settings at any time.

Notifications

Workout reminders are scheduled on your device. No notification data is sent to us or anyone else.

Analytics, ads and tracking

The app has no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking.

Children

Because the app doesn't collect personal data, it doesn't knowingly collect data from children or anyone else.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
