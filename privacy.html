Privacy Policy

Pushup Tracker · Last updated October 7, 2026

Short version: your workouts stay on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. We only receive data if you choose to join the optional leaderboard, and then only your account, nickname and scores. We never sell data or use it for ads.

Your workout data

Your reps, goals, streaks, notes, challenges and settings are stored on your device. A copy is kept in the app's private storage on your iPhone so nothing is lost if iOS clears temporary data. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it, except for your iCloud backup (below).

iCloud backup

If iCloud backup is on (it is by default, and you can turn it off in the app's Settings), your workout data is saved to your own iCloud account so you can restore it on a new phone. It is stored by Apple in your account; we can't access it.

Leaderboard and sign-in (optional)

The leaderboard is optional. If you join it, you sign in with Apple or with Google, and we store the following on our leaderboard service, which is hosted by Supabase:

  • an account ID, the email address from Apple or Google (Apple lets you hide your real email), and the basic profile details they share, such as your name
  • the nickname you choose
  • your leaderboard scores: reps this week, total reps, best set and current streak
  • reports you send about other players' nicknames
  • standard technical logs, such as IP addresses and sign-in times, which the service keeps for security

Other players can see only your nickname and scores. We use this data only to run the leaderboard and to deal with reports of offensive names. We don't sell it, share it for advertising, or use it to track you.

Google sign-in: if you sign in with Google, the app only requests your basic profile and email address, and uses them only to create and identify your leaderboard account. We don't access any other Google data. Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data: in the app, open the Ranks tab and tap Leave leaderboard. That permanently deletes your leaderboard account, email, nickname and scores from our service. Your workouts on your phone are not affected.

Apple Health and Apple Watch

If you turn on Save to Apple Health, finished workouts are saved to the Health app. During a workout, the Apple Watch app reads your heart rate and active calories to show them live and saves them with the workout in Health. Health data stays on your devices and in Apple Health; it is never sent to us or to the leaderboard, and never used for advertising.

Camera

If you turn on camera counting, the camera is used to detect your body position and count reps. The video is processed live on your device. It is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

Location and weather

If you allow location access, the app looks up the current temperature and humidity when you start a workout. To do this, your location coordinates are sent to the free weather service Open-Meteo for that single request. The weather reading and location are saved on your device with your sets. We don't receive your location. Location access is optional and you can turn it off in iOS Settings at any time.

Notifications

Workout reminders are scheduled on your device. No notification data is sent to us.

Analytics, ads and tracking

The app has no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking.

Children

The leaderboard is not intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has joined, contact us and we will delete the account.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: visit the support section.
